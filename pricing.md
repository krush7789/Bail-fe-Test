---

## Understanding the Three Price Columns

Every service has three pricing options within each tier. Here is what each one means:

**One-time**
The client pays this once. You build and deliver the product. Job done. No further obligation on either side. The client owns the setup. If anything needs updating later, they contact you again and pay separately.

**Monthly**
A retainer the client pays every month *after* the one-time setup — for maintenance, small updates, support, and hosting management. The client can cancel anytime. This is your recurring revenue and the backbone of the business.

**Annual**
A bundled deal. Instead of paying the one-time fee now and then the monthly fee every month, the client pays one flat amount upfront for the full year.

The formula: **one-time + 10 months of retainer = annual price.** This means the client gets **2 months free** for committing annually instead of month-to-month. For purely monthly services (no one-time fee), annual = 10 × monthly.

**Example — AI Chatbots, Starter tier:**

| Option | Price | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| One-time | ₹19,999 | Bot built and delivered. No ongoing commitment. |
| Monthly | ₹3,500/month | Ongoing support retainer after setup. Cancel anytime. |
| Annual | ₹54,999 | ₹19,999 + 10×₹3,500 = ₹54,999. Client saves ₹7,001 vs. paying monthly. |

**Which option is for whom:**

| Option | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| One-time | Client just wants it built, no ongoing support needed |
| Monthly | Client wants support but not ready to commit long-term |
| Annual | Client commits for a year, gets 2 months free; you get guaranteed cashflow |

## Pricing Philosophy

| Tier | Goal | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Win first clients, cover tool costs + minimum labor | No profit expected. Every Starter client = a case study. |
| **Standard** | Your real margin. This is where the business lives. | Target 60–70% of all closed deals. |
| **Premium** | Anchor pricing. Shows range and ambition. | Rarely close as a new agency — that's fine. |

**Annual formula:** one-time + 10 × monthly (2 months free). For monthly-only services: 10 × monthly.
